What is the ABHA Number & Address?

The Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) is a unique 14-digit identifier that links all your health records across hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, and digital health lockers across India.

14-Digit ABHA Number
National health identity number (e.g. 14-8291-5021-9482).
ABHA Address (PHR Handle)
Easy-to-remember handle like an email (e.g. name@abdm).
Your data remains 100% encrypted. No hospital can view records without your explicit consent.
